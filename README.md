### Notable Family Members

- **Sire - King Kamehameha:** One of Japan's most successful stallions, leading sire in Japan multiple times
- **Dam - Admire Groove:** Japanese Horse of the Year (2003), won the Victoria Mile and other G1 races
- **Maternal Grandsire - Sunday Silence:** Legendary American-bred stallion, revolutionized Japanese racing bloodlines
- **Maternal Granddam - Air Groove:** Japanese Horse of the Year (1997), won the Japanese Oaks and Tenno Sho

### Racing Career Highlights

Duramente had a distinguished racing career in Japan:
- **2016 Tokyo Yūshun (Japanese Derby):** Winner
- **2015 Mainichi Hai:** Winner
- Established himself as one of the premier Japanese racehorses of his generation
- Retired to stud following his racing career

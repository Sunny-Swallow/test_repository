### Notable Ancestry

The pedigree features several legendary bloodlines:

1. **Northern Dancer** - Appears in both paternal and maternal lines, one of the most influential sires in Thoroughbred racing history
2. **Nijinsky II** - Winner of the English Triple Crown (1970)
3. **Sharastani** - Winner of the Epsom Derby (1986)

### Racing Career Highlights

Biwa Hayahide was foaled on March 10, 1990, and became one of Japan's most celebrated racehorses, winning multiple Grade 1 races including the Japanese Derby (Tokyo Yushun) in 1993.

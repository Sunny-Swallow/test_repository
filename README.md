- **Country**: Japan

### Racing Achievements
Mayano Top Gun was one of Japan's most successful racehorses in the late 1990s. His major victories include:
- Japan Cup (1997)
- Arima Kinen/Grand Prix (1997)
- Tenno Sho (Spring) (1997, 1998)
- Takarazuka Kinen (1997)

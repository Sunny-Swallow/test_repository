### Racing Legacy

Rice Shower is remembered as one of Japan's greatest stayers, with notable victories including multiple Tenno Sho (Spring) championships. His pedigree combines international bloodlines through Realizado with strong Japanese foundation stock through Lady Broker, contributing to his exceptional stamina and competitive spirit.

## Pedigree of Biwa Hayahide

Biwa Hayahide (ビワハヤヒデ) was a Japanese Thoroughbred racehorse, foaled in 1990. He was one of the most successful racehorses in Japanese racing history.

### Ancestral Lineage

**Sire Line:**
- **Sire:** Sharrood (シャルード)
  - **Paternal Grandsire:** Damascus
  - **Paternal Granddam:** Bolling Brook

**Dam Line:**
- **Dam:** Pacificus (パシフィカス)
  - **Maternal Grandsire:** Northern Dancer
  - **Maternal Granddam:** Reassert

### Notable Achievements
- Won the Japanese Triple Crown in 1993
- Winner of multiple Grade 1 races including Satsuki Sho, Tokyo Yushun (Japanese Derby), and Kikuka Sho
